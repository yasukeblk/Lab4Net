Lab4Net

Learn the CCNA by doing it.

Lab4Net is a free CCNA 200-301 lab simulator that runs in your browser. Configure routers, switches and PCs from IOS-style consoles, watch packets cross the network map, and get graded on whether the network actually works.

The labs

77 hands-on labs mapped to the CCNA 200-301 exam topics: 38 builds, 37 troubleshooting incidents and 2 capstones, with 56 lessons that teach the idea before the commands.

The console

Consoles that answer like IOS: ? help, Tab completion, the ^ error marker, passwords, Telnet and SSH, and live ping and traceroute output.

The map

Every ping travels the map along the path it really takes. When it fails, the map shows where and says why.

Your progress is saved in this browser only. Settings › Data exports a backup to move it. This public site counts visits anonymously: no cookies and no personal data, and nothing at all with Do Not Track on.
